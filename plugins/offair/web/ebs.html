Off Air

Off Air plays when a channel has no scheduled media, when playback fails, or when a weather capture errors. Choose the video and the audio independently — color bars with a beep, or a slate with background music.

Playout is not built yet, so these settings are stored for when it lands.

Custom slates

Upload your own off-air slate per region, exactly as v1.0.0's EBS slates worked — a custom upload is what plays, and the bundled stock slate fills in when a region has none. The preview shows the effective slate for the region and aspect.

Offline screens

The slates shown when a channel is off air — carried over from the EBS plugin's v1 media. Each region and aspect ratio has its own.
