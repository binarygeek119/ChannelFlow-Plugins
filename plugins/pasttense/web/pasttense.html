Past Tense News

Point ChannelFlow at a folder of news events. Each event is its own folder — 1992 LA Riots News coverage, Apollo 11 News coverage — and holds that event's coverage videos (1992 Los Angeles riots news coverage.mkv, NBC News Coverage of Apollo 11 Part 11.mkv). Every event becomes one Past Tense News item, shown on the Media page like a TV show without seasons. A poster.jpg / poster.png in the event folder is its artwork.

Type a path, or click through the folders below to find the one holding the event folders.

News folders

Each added folder is scanned into the media database as its own Past Tense News items.
